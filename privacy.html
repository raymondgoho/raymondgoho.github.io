Privacy Policy

YouTube Likes Sync · Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the YouTube Likes Sync application accesses, why it accesses it, how long it is kept, and how it can be removed. It is written for the one person who uses the app: its developer, Raymond Goho.

1. What the app is

YouTube Likes Sync is a personal, single-user automation that runs once a day on the developer's own computer. Its only purpose is to keep a permanent, searchable archive of the videos that the developer has "liked" on YouTube. The app is not distributed, sold, or made available to the public.

2. Data accessed from Google

The app requests exactly one Google permission (scope): https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. With this permission the app can read:

  • the list of videos the signed-in account has "liked" on YouTube,
  • for each of those videos: the title, the channel name, the publish date, the duration, and the video link.

The app requests no other scope. It is read-only: it cannot upload, post, comment, modify, delete or subscribe to anything on the account. It does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Photos, or any other Google service.

3. Why the data is accessed

The sole purpose is to maintain a personal archive of liked videos so they can be browsed and searched later. This data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

4. Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally in a private database on the developer's own computer. The app has no server, no cloud storage, and no web-hosted copy of the data. Nothing collected through this app is transmitted to any third party, is shared, sold, or published.

5. How long the data is kept

Locally stored records are kept indefinitely as a personal archive. The Google login token is stored in a file on the same computer, readable only by the developer's own user account.

6. Who can use the app

Only the developer's own Google account (rymngh@gmail.com) is authorized while the app is in testing, and only that account will use it in production. No other user can or will sign in.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing "YouTube Likes Sync" from the list of connected apps. Locally stored records can be deleted by deleting the local database file on the computer.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: rymngh@gmail.com.
